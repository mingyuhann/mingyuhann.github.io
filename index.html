Mingyu Han is a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering at KAIST in Wearable and Interactive Technology Lab. He's working closely with Prof. Ian Oakley and Prof. Xucong Zhang. His doctoral research centers on human gaze as a foundation for novel computing paradigms. He builds eye trackers from scratch, collects real-world gaze data, and envisions how such data can transform the way humans interact with robots and AI systems. Outside the lab, he enjoys playing jazz standards.
